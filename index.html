The problem

Waste heat is next door. The deal is hard to see.

An apartment developer planning heating has to answer three questions before a data-center heat deal is real. Today each one takes consultants and weeks.

01 · Discover

Who has heat to export?

Which nearby data center actually has recoverable heat, and is willing to sell it?

02 · Engineer

Does the physics fit?

Temperature, demand, pipe route and backup systems all have to line up.

03 · Justify

Do the numbers work?

Avoided heating cost has to beat the investment and the cost of running it.
